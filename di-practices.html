D&I Practices

Welcome to the D&I Practices page. Here you will find information and resources related to diversity and inclusion practices within the Python community.

Best Practices

We encourage the adoption of the following best practices to promote diversity and inclusion:

  • Foster an inclusive environment where everyone feels welcome and valued.
  • Promote diverse representation in leadership and decision-making roles.
  • Provide equal opportunities for participation and growth.
  • Address and prevent discrimination, harassment, and bias.
  • Encourage open and respectful communication.
